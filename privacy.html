Gmail Cleaner Buddy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Gmail Cleaner Buddy Android and iOS apps ("the apps," "we") process information when you use them to find the senders filling up your mailbox and clean their messages.

1. Information We Access

The apps access your Google account data only through the official Gmail API, after you grant explicit permission.

  • Scope of access: we request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify scope, which allows reading message metadata and moving messages to Trash.
  • Data processed: your account address, and each message's ID, sender (From header) and size estimate. The apps never read the subject or body of your messages.

2. How We Use Your Data

This data is used only for the apps' core features:

  • To count and display the senders filling up your mailbox.
  • To move messages from the senders you choose to Trash, only after you confirm.

3. Data Storage and Retention

We have no servers: your data is not sent to us. The apps keep a local cache on your device.

  • To avoid re-reading your whole mailbox on every analysis, the apps store your account address and the ID, sender and size of each message in a cache on your device. Subjects and bodies are never stored.
  • The cache never leaves your device and is excluded from device backups. It is deleted when you sign out or uninstall the app. On iOS, the system may also clear it if storage runs low.
  • Google access tokens, and on iOS the refresh token, are kept only in memory and are not written to the device. Access tokens expire in about one hour.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information or email data with third parties. The apps connect only to Google's own services.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Gmail Cleaner Buddy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Security

All traffic to Google uses HTTPS. Because we do not host your data, its security relies on Google's infrastructure and your own device. Emails are moved to Gmail's Trash (recoverable for 30 days) and are never permanently deleted by the apps.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be shown on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the project repository: github.com/mbastida43/gmailcleanerbuddyapp.
